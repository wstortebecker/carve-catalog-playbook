How Carve works

A continuously working commerce intelligence system.

Carve maintains a living understanding of the catalog, addressable demand, search visibility, result selection and PDP performance. Agents explore every product, page, query and market; investigate opportunities; make permitted changes; and learn from what happens.

The whole system

Evidence enters. Understanding persists. Agents reason and act. Outcomes become new evidence.

The outer lifecycle is durable. Inside each case, the work is adaptive: Carve can revisit any question, use different tools, keep competing explanations alive or ask the retailer for missing truth.

The active case

Question, evidence, beliefs, decisions and history stay together.

These are reasoning moves, not stages. The agent can use any of them repeatedly, in any order, and retain several live explanations.

Frame the performance question

Define the product or page, demand, market, device, surface, cohort, comparator and outcome precisely enough to reason about.

Form competing mechanisms

Ask what could produce the pattern, what each explanation predicts and what evidence would weaken it.

Seek decision-changing evidence

Join first-party history, external observations, live experience, customer evidence and expert research only where it separates actions.

Inspect the real experience

See the actual SERP, Shopping result, landing page or PDP in the relevant market and device—not only an aggregate metric.

Maintain the current performance read

State the best-supported limit, viable alternatives, uncertainty, missing truth and why a particular move is or is not justified.

Design an intervention and its test

Make the smallest coherent change that improves or tests the likely mechanism; predefine expected response, comparator and guardrails.

Four operating layers

Persistent knowledge, exhaustive coverage, adaptive intelligence and governed action.

These layers explain what the software must continuously do. They are not screens or a fixed order of work.

01

Grounding and memory

Turns disconnected source records into an inspectable living world model.

  • Identity: products, pages, queries, offers, markets and observations resolve to connected entities.
  • Authority: retailer facts remain distinct from observations, derived metrics and model beliefs.
  • History: raw evidence, transformations, decisions, changes and outcomes remain attributable.
  • Missing systems: available evidence and confidence change; reasoning adapts.
02

Exploration and coordination

Provides continuous coverage without inheriting human attention limits.

  • Coverage: repeatedly inspect every relevant product, page, query cluster, surface and market.
  • Opportunity: look for upside even when nothing is broken or declining.
  • Case registry: merge duplicates, split different mechanisms and share evidence.
  • Collision control: coordinate simultaneous work that touches the same commercial object.
03

Adaptive intelligence

Combines general reasoning with deep ecommerce expertise and real tools.

  • Reasoning: frame ambiguity, generate alternatives and update beliefs from evidence.
  • Expertise: SEO, Shopping, content, merchandising, CRO and market mechanisms guide inquiry.
  • Specialists: delegate bounded research, analysis, creative and implementation work in parallel.
  • Tool choice: use the source most likely to change the decision—not every available integration.
04

Governed action and learning

Makes intelligence useful without detaching it from truth, authority or outcomes.

  • Collaboration: ask for missing facts, intent and approvals when judgment belongs to the retailer.
  • Execution: draft, preview, stage, test, publish, verify and reverse within permission.
  • Measurement: observe the commercial result, expected mechanism, comparator and guardrails.
  • Learning: update the case, retailer memory and future judgment from corrections and results.

How agent intelligence behaves

The playbook guides questions. It does not dictate a route.

One orchestrated intelligence

Carve asks: what would most change what I believe or what I do next?

A case does not automatically call every integration. The agent uses the world model, opens the most diagnostic source, inspects the real experience, revises its explanations and delegates bounded work to specialists when useful.

The public worktree is seeded ecommerce expertise—not a deterministic route. Retailer facts, live evidence, corrections and measured outcomes continually change how that expertise is applied.

System boundary

What Carve is—and what it deliberately is not.

Carve is

  • A persistent model of the retailer’s catalog, addressable demand, markets, evidence and change history.
  • Continuous catalog-wide exploration plus adaptive case intelligence—not attention-limited triage.
  • General reasoning combined with ecommerce worktrees, specialist agents and real research and execution tools.
  • Adaptive to missing systems: it uses the available evidence and makes the resulting uncertainty explicit.
  • Permissioned: autonomous in one action class, approval-bound in another and collaborative when truth or intent is missing.

Carve is not

  • A deterministic checklist or one universal route through every performance issue.
  • A system that equates connector data with truth or treats rankings, recordings and competitor patterns as causal proof.
  • A model allowed to invent product facts, silently decide commercial policy or act beyond its connected capability.
  • A system that only detects breakage; its purpose is to find and create unrealized performance.
  • Currently a post-purchase, inventory-planning, paid-media or unit-economics system; those remain context or routed work.
The system in one sentence

Carve gives the whole catalog and every addressable demand opportunity a continuously working ecommerce expert.

General intelligence supplies the reasoning. Ecommerce expertise, connected evidence, persistent memory, tools, permissions and measured outcomes make it dependable and useful.
