How Carve works.

Carve connects retailer truth and shopper behaviour with outside-market evidence, finds unrealized performance, investigates each case adaptively, makes permitted changes and learns from measured outcomes.

02

Raw data becomes a scoped signal—not a diagnosis.

Carve resolves the entity and cohort, computes the relevant metric or pattern, compares it with a meaningful expectation, and stores the gap with its evidence and limitations. It then opens or updates a case.

Every signal contains

Entity or demand cluster · market · language · device · surface · traffic cohort · metric and denominator · baseline or comparator · magnitude · commercial upside · source records · freshness · confidence · limitations · detection time · related case.

Question opened

What prevents comprehension, confidence, product/variant choice or technically successful addition?

Carve does not only detect drops or errors. Stable performance can still contain upside when relevant demand, matched products, customer criteria or competitors show that the experience can be materially better.
Market is a dimension, not a fifth problem. The same product can have different demand, language, SERP, assortment, offer, competitors and behaviour by country; Carve retains those differences from detection through measurement.

Adaptive case intelligence

What would most change the belief or the action?

The agent keeps distinct explanations alive, states what each predicts, seeks evidence that separates them, and stops only when an attractive move is justified—or more evidence is unlikely to change the decision.

Reframe, split or merge scope

Change product, page, query, market, device, surface or cohort when the aggregate hides different mechanisms.

Generate and challenge mechanisms

Ask what could produce the pattern, what each explanation predicts, and what evidence would weaken it.

Select decision-changing evidence

Choose the source, live observation or retailer question most likely to separate actions—not every available tool.

04

The investigation library is deep. The route stays adaptive.

These are the ecommerce mechanisms and evidence families Carve can draw from. They are not mandatory checks, and technical plumbing is only one possible explanation—not the centre of the work.

Demand coverage

Uncaptured demand

Does valuable local demand match something the retailer can credibly serve, and is the right page missing or merely expressed badly?

Carve investigates

  • Query jobs, intent, local vocabulary, demand level, trend, seasonality and the page types Google rewards.
  • Catalog and product truth, market availability, assortment depth and whether the retailer can genuinely fulfil the job.
  • Existing product, collection, category and guide pages; what each currently targets; overlap and cannibalization.
  • Competitor landing pages, content, merchandising, product mix and offer needed to satisfy the demand.

Candidate mechanisms

  • The demand is not targeted; an existing page never states the job; the wrong page type owns it; page roles overlap; the market assortment or offer is not credible.

A justified change can look like

  • Create a collection, landing page or guide with the right page role and product set.
  • Retarget an existing page; write its title, H1, collection/body copy and merchandising around the job.
  • Expose existing assortment through navigation and internal links; separate or consolidate overlapping pages.
  • Hold when the retailer cannot credibly satisfy the demand—rather than manufacture relevance.
Organic visibility

Weak rank or impressions

A suitable page exists. What prevents it from becoming the strongest relevant answer for the intended query job?

Carve investigates

  • Query-to-page ownership and actual ranking by market/device; live SERP intent, result type and competing pages.
  • Title, H1, body and collection text, product description, specs, use cases, imagery, product data and how completely the page answers the job.
  • Page role, differentiation from sibling pages, cannibalization, internal linking and merchandising prominence.
  • Competitor usefulness, assortment and offer; eligibility, rendering, indexing or feed issues only when evidence points there.

Candidate mechanisms

  • Intent mismatch; weak or generic product expression; unclear page role; shallow content/assortment; cannibalization; low internal prominence; stronger competitive answer; limited eligibility.

A justified change can look like

  • Rewrite positioning, title, H1, description, collection copy, specs or supporting decision content.
  • Differentiate sibling pages, consolidate overlap when warranted or create a supporting page for a distinct intent.
  • Add relevant internal references and expose the page in the right collection/navigation context.
  • Correct product/feed/structured information or technical eligibility only where it explains the visibility gap.
Result selection

Weak organic or free-Shopping CTR

Given the query, position, market, device and surface, why does the shopper choose another result?

Carve investigates

  • CTR conditioned on query and position mix—not a blended average—and the exact page/offer shown.
  • The rendered organic snippet or product listing: title, product name, description/snippet, image, variant, price, stock, shipping, reviews and badges.
  • Search intent and landing-page promise; feed/page consistency; richer-result eligibility; competitor presentation and real offer differences.
  • Market/device differences and sampled carousel placement where native reporting cannot show the slot.

Candidate mechanisms

  • Lower-intent query mix; weaker position; unclear or generic title; wrong page or variant; poor imagery; missing product data; lost enhanced presentation; genuinely weaker offer.

A justified change can look like

  • Rewrite the title/meta or product title to express the right product, use and differentiator in the visible result.
  • Select or brief a stronger primary image; correct variant, price, availability, shipping or review data across page and feed.
  • Clarify which page should appear and align its on-page promise with the query.
  • Route real price, stock or shipping weakness instead of disguising it with copy.
Product decision

Weak PDP decision or ATC

What prevents a qualified shopper from understanding, trusting, choosing or successfully adding this product?

Carve investigates

  • Traffic intent and promise continuity from search/listing to PDP; buyability, stock and variant availability for the exact cohort.
  • First-screen positioning, product description, facts, specs, use cases, comparison, sizing, variants, imagery/video, reviews and proof.
  • Price, promotion, delivery, returns and local offer; customer questions from search, reviews, support and surveys.
  • Replays, heatmaps and funnels; selection/CTA/valid-add behaviour; mobile hierarchy, errors, speed, template releases and competitor PDPs.

Candidate mechanisms

  • Wrong traffic; unclear value; missing decision information; weak proof/imagery; unavailable choice; unattractive offer; interaction failure; mobile regression; product-market mismatch.

A justified change can look like

  • Sharpen positioning and rewrite the product description around the customer job and approved differentiators.
  • Add or reorder decision facts, comparison, fit/size/use-case guidance, proof and imagery.
  • Clarify delivery/returns and selection state; repair variant or valid-add behaviour; recommend a better alternative.
  • Route price, stock, product, imagery or engineering work with an exact evidence-backed brief.
The important principle: Carve is trying to improve performance, not merely prove that data is clean. Validation is proportionate to the decision. Once the evidence is trustworthy enough, attention moves to product expression, page usefulness, result presentation, shopper decision support and the real competitive experience.
05

Agent dispositions, performance outcomes and learning are separate.

“Apply” is an operational action. “CTR improved” is a performance outcome. “Expand” is a learning decision. Keeping them separate prevents the system from confusing activity with impact.

AskResolve missing truth or intent

A precise retailer question with the disputed fact, why it matters and which decision the answer unlocks.

PrepareCreate a decision-ready change

Exact before/after content, data, asset brief or implementation with rationale, preview, dependencies and measurement.

Test / applyUse an authorized write surface

Stage, experiment or publish within permission; record the version and verify the rendered live state.

RouteHand the right owner exact work

Evidence, mechanism, recommended change, commercial choice, dependencies and approval—not a vague ticket.

Hold / monitorNo justified move yet

Retain the case and reopen when evidence, demand, catalog state, authority or the expected value changes.

ReverseRestore the controlled prior state

Rollback because verification failed, a pre-set harm trigger fired or measured evidence no longer supports the intervention.

Every intervention carries the same contract:

Diagnosed mechanism · exact before/after · affected products/pages/markets · why behaviour should change · dependencies · authority and approval · preview · target performance outcome · expected mechanism response · comparator · maturation window · guardrails · live verification · rollback.

Measurement and learning

Did reality respond as predicted?

Carve uses the strongest feasible comparison: controlled experiment where appropriate, otherwise matched cohorts, annotated before/after and converging mechanism evidence. It accounts for maturation time, overlapping changes and market/device heterogeneity.

1. Verify live stateConfirm the intended version rendered correctly in every affected market/device and no dependency failed.
2. Observe mechanismDid relevance, presentation, comprehension, selection or valid-add behaviour move in the predicted direction?
3. Measure outcomeUse the exact cohort, baseline/counterfactual, evidence window, minimum evidence and harm guardrails.
4. Update judgmentStore result and uncertainty; update case, signal calibration, retailer memory and related cohort hypotheses.
